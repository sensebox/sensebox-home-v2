senseBox:home

Service mode

Sensors and LoRaWAN are paused: nothing is measured or sent while service mode runs. Settings changed here apply after the restart. Service mode closes after - without activity.

This WiFi still uses the default password. Set your own under Settings: only then does the box keep its WiFi open during normal operation, so you can reach service mode without opening the case.
